---
description: Rename a peer, or change what you are called
argument-hint: "<current> <new>   or   me <new>"
allowed-tools: Bash(${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/bin/crosstalk:*)
---

!`"${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/bin/crosstalk" cli rename $ARGUMENTS`

Names are local. What you call someone is yours to choose and they never see it,
so rename freely. Renaming yourself only changes how you introduce yourself to
people you pair with from now on; anyone you have already paired with keeps the
name they gave you.

The key does not change, so the fingerprint stays the same and nothing has to be
paired again.
